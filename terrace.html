Terrace

Web3 · Campaign · 2025 · Lead Product Designer

A Web3 loot box campaign experience designed to drive engagement and onboard new users into a gamified on-chain rewards ecosystem.

Overview

Terrace is a Web3 loot box campaign platform — randomized reward drops that drive on-chain participation. The challenge was making it feel genuinely exciting while staying accessible to users new to Web3.

Every interaction was choreographed around tension and reveal. I covered the full scope: user flows, component system, operator dashboard, and the end-user claim experience.
