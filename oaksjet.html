Oaksjet

Travel · Luxury · 2025 · Lead Product Designer

A private jet booking platform for the Nigerian market — designed for both mobile and desktop, with a dedicated operator profile experience for charter companies.

Overview

Oaksjet makes chartering a private jet feel as simple as booking a commercial flight — built for Nigeria's high-net-worth market across mobile and desktop. Mobile focused on speed: route, jet, confirm. Desktop opened up a richer experience for corporate travel managers.

The operator profile was equally important — a trust surface for first-time users unfamiliar with private aviation, showcasing fleet, credentials, and past routes.
