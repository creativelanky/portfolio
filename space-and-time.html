Dreamspace

Web3 · AI · 2024 · Product Designer

A Web3-native data platform that brings verifiable, AI-powered analytics to smart contracts and on-chain applications at scale.

Overview

Dreamspace is a Web3-native data platform — verifiable, AI-powered analytics for smart contracts and on-chain apps. The product had to serve two very different audiences: developers needing SQL query interfaces, and business stakeholders needing readable dashboards.

I built a layered information architecture that puts clear summaries up front, with technical depth one click deeper. I also led the developer docs and API key management flows.
